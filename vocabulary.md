Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are a deep learning architecture that involves two competing AI models tasked with generating highly realistic data (such as text, video, audio, or images) based on an initial training dataset. These models subsequently evaluate each other’s output, attempting to determine whether the data is new—generated by the opposing network—or part of the original dataset.
