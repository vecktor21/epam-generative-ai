GenAI Vocabulary – Usage Relationship Graph

Relationships between the 20 vocabulary terms (vocabulary.md) and the five report sections (research.md). Explicit frequency = the term itself (or a direct spelling variant) appears in the section. Implicit frequency = the concept is used through a synonym or related phrase without naming the term. Edge thickness and node size reflect the frequency in the selected mode. Statistics are computed by graph_stats.py (regex matching over the report text, exported to graph_data.js). Dashed green edges are term–term co-occurrence (both terms used in the same section; weight = number of shared sections). Hover for exact numbers, drag nodes to rearrange.
